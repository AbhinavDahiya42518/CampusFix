ABOUT

Why CampusFix?

On most campuses, student issues vanish into suggestion boxes, WhatsApp groups and forgotten emails. There's no visibility, no tracking, and no accountability.

CampusFix brings a single, transparent pipeline for every campus problem — from a flickering tube-light to a leaking washroom. Every report gets a unique ID, a visible status, and a clear path to resolution.

The Problem

Reports get lost. Students never know if their complaint reached the right team. Maintenance has no central view of what's urgent.

Our Solution

A unified reporting + tracking layer: structured categories, unique complaint IDs, real-time status, and a dashboard for campus teams.

The Mission

Make every student voice count. Turn passive complaints into measurable, transparent campus improvement — one resolved issue at a time.
