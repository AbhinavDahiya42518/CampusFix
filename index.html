How it works

From a report to a resolved campus.

Three steps. One complaint ID. A record you can open again on this device.

  1. 01

    Report an issue

    Describe what is wrong, where it is, and which campus system it affects. CampusFix assigns a complaint ID immediately.

  2. 02

    Track its progress

    Return with that ID to see whether the complaint is pending, in progress, or resolved.

  3. 03

    Get it resolved

    Follow the complaint through to closure. In this demo, status lives in your browser so the full loop can be shown without a server.

Issue categories

The problems students actually meet.

Choose a category to start a report. Each card opens the form with that category selected.

Electricity

Outages, flickering lights, faulty fans, and unsafe sockets.

Water Supply

Low pressure, dry washrooms, leaks, and hostel supply cuts.

Wi-Fi & Internet

Dead zones, dropping connections, and ports that never come up.

Cleanliness

Overflowing bins, uncleaned corridors, and washroom hygiene.

Infrastructure

Broken furniture, damaged paths, doors, and shared campus spaces.

Classroom Facilities

Projectors, boards, seating, fans, and rooms that are not ready to teach in.

A complaint ID, then a status.

  3. The complaint is saved as Pending.

About CampusFix

Maintenance should not disappear into a chat thread.

A college hackathon project for reporting campus issues and keeping every complaint visible until it is closed.

The problem

Requests get lost.

A dark corridor, a dry washroom, or a dead network port often lives in a paper slip or an informal message. Students rarely know whether anyone is working on it.

The solution

One desk for the campus.

CampusFix collects electricity, water, connectivity, cleanliness, infrastructure, and classroom reports. Each one receives an ID and a status a student can check again.

The mission

Make campus care visible.

Give students a voice that stays on the record. This version proves the experience in the browser. It is not an official facilities system and it has no backend.
